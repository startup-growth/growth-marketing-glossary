Experiments and measurement

Testing hypotheses, measuring impact and understanding behavior over time.

  • Growth Experimentation

    Systematic testing of ideas to discover effective growth interventions.

  • A/B Testing

    Randomized comparison of two or more experiences to estimate their effects.

  • Incrementality Testing

    Measurement of additional outcomes caused by a marketing intervention.

  • Cohort Analysis

    Analysis of groups sharing a starting event or characteristic over time.

  • Marketing Attribution

    Assignment of conversion credit to marketing interactions.

  • Conversion Rate

    Percentage of eligible people completing a defined action.

  • Funnel Analysis

    Measurement of how people progress through a sequence of actions.

  • Experiment Guardrail Metrics

    Measures that detect unwanted harm from an experimental change.

  • Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)

    Estimation of channel contributions from aggregate marketing and sales data.
