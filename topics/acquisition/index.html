Acquisition and channels

Winning appropriate customers through paid media, search and demand generation.

  • Customer Acquisition

    Process of converting potential buyers into customers.

  • Performance Marketing

    Marketing managed primarily against measurable actions and economic results.

  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

    Improving organic search visibility and relevant search traffic.

  • Demand Generation

    Activities that create awareness, interest, and demand for a product.
