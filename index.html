Growth Marketing Glossary

33 growth marketing terms, explained with clear definitions and practical examples.

Use this A to Z reference to understand the metrics, frameworks, and methods behind customer acquisition, activation, retention, and sustainable growth. Each term links to a detailed guide showing how the concept works in practice.

What is growth marketing?

Growth marketing is the work of getting more of the right people to become successful customers. It looks beyond campaigns to the whole customer experience, from finding a product and reaching first value to staying, buying again, or recommending it. Growth teams use experiments and measurement to find which changes improve those outcomes.

Growth marketing terms and definitions A to Z

33 terms

  • A/B Testing

    Randomized comparison of two or more experiences to estimate their effects.

  • AARRR Framework

    Acquisition, activation, retention, referral, and revenue framework.

  • Activation Rate

    Percentage of eligible users reaching a defined first-value milestone.

  • CAC Payback Period

    Time needed to recover customer acquisition cost through gross profit.

  • Churn Rate

    Share of customers or recurring revenue lost during a period.

  • Cohort Analysis

    Analysis of groups sharing a starting event or characteristic over time.

  • Conversion Rate

    Percentage of eligible people completing a defined action.

  • Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

    Improving the percentage of visitors completing a desired action.

  • Customer Acquisition

    Process of converting potential buyers into customers.

  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

    Average acquisition expenditure per new customer.

  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLV/LTV)

    Estimated economic value generated by a customer throughout the relationship.

  • Customer Retention

    Ability to keep customers active and purchasing over time.

  • Demand Generation

    Activities that create awareness, interest, and demand for a product.

  • Experiment Guardrail Metrics

    Measures that detect unwanted harm from an experimental change.

  • Funnel Analysis

    Measurement of how people progress through a sequence of actions.

  • Growth Experimentation

    Systematic testing of ideas to discover effective growth interventions.

  • Growth Funnel

    Model of customer progression through acquisition and conversion stages.

  • Growth Loops

    Reinforcing systems where the output of one cycle generates future growth.

  • Growth Marketing

    Data-driven marketing that optimizes growth throughout the customer lifecycle.

  • Growth Strategy

    Coordinated plan for achieving sustainable business growth.

  • Incrementality Testing

    Measurement of additional outcomes caused by a marketing intervention.

  • Lifecycle Marketing

    Stage-specific marketing designed to advance and retain customers.

  • LTV:CAC Ratio

    Relationship between customer lifetime value and acquisition cost.

  • Marketing Attribution

    Assignment of conversion credit to marketing interactions.

  • Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)

    Estimation of channel contributions from aggregate marketing and sales data.

  • Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

    Retention of recurring revenue from existing customers, including expansion.

  • North Star Metric

    Primary metric representing customer value creation and long-term growth potential.

  • Performance Marketing

    Marketing managed primarily against measurable actions and economic results.

  • Product-Led Growth (PLG)

    Growth strategy where product experience drives adoption and expansion.

  • Product-Market Fit

    Degree to which a product satisfies meaningful market demand.

  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

    Improving organic search visibility and relevant search traffic.

  • Time to Value (TTV)

    Time until new customers receive a meaningful benefit from a product.

  • Unit Economics

    Revenue and cost relationships at a customer or transaction level.
