# Funnel Analysis

Funnel analysis measures how people move through a defined sequence of actions toward a goal. It can show where users stop progressing, but the drop-off alone does not tell you why.

## Where do people leave a signup funnel?

Imagine a hypothetical collaboration app with the following seven-day funnel:

| Step | People reaching it | Rate from prior step |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Visit signup page | 4,000 | Starting population |
| Create account | 1,200 | 30% |
| Start setup | 600 | 50% |
| Complete first useful shared task | 240 | 40% |

Only **240 ÷ 4,000 = 6%** of the original visitors reach the final step. Among those who start setup, 40% complete the first useful task.

This is a lead for investigation. Some visitors may be ineligible, some may need a coworker's approval, and some may encounter a broken invitation flow.

## How do you investigate a funnel drop-off?

Inspect failed events, session recordings, and customer questions at the step where people stop. Compare mobile and desktop, acquisition sources, and types of accounts.

If mobile users abandon setup more often, look at the actual interface before changing the signup copy. A hidden button or failed authentication step may be the cause.

Define the counting rules. Does each person enter once? Must events occur in order? Does everyone get the same amount of time to finish? Without consistent rules, the funnel can give a false comparison.

## How is funnel analysis different from cohort analysis?

[A growth funnel](https://startup-growth.github.io/growth-marketing-glossary/terms/growth-funnel/) describes the stages of a journey. Funnel analysis measures movement through them. [Cohort analysis](https://startup-growth.github.io/growth-marketing-glossary/terms/cohort-analysis/) compares groups who started at different times or share a characteristic.

The final step should reflect a useful outcome, not just activity. See [activation rate](https://startup-growth.github.io/growth-marketing-glossary/terms/activation-rate/) and [CRO](https://startup-growth.github.io/growth-marketing-glossary/terms/conversion-rate-optimization-cro/) for why the chosen event matters.
